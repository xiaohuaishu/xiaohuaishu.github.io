🔬 研究方向与成果

大语言模型

预训练、后训练、推理加速、长上下文理解、量化与压缩

  • HoPE: A Novel Positional Encoding Without Long-Term Decay for Enhanced Context Awareness and Extrapolation
    Yuhan Chen, Ang Lv, Jian Luan, Bin Wang, Wei Liu
    ACL 2025 CCF A类 SAC Highlight

    提出高频旋转位置编码，打破传统RoPE的长期衰减限制，在长上下文理解任务中展现出更强的外推能力和鲁棒性。

  • TailorKV: A Hybrid Framework for Long-Context Inference via Tailored KV Cache Optimization
    Dingyu Yao, Bowen Shen, Zheng Lin, Wei Liu, Jian Luan, Bin Wang, Weiping Wang
    ACL 2025 CCF A类

    开发混合压缩方法，将量化与卸载无缝集成，在激进压缩设置下实现几乎无损的性能，支持128k上下文在单GPU上部署。

  • More is not always better? Enhancing Many-Shot In-Context Learning with Differentiated and Reweighting Objectives
    Xiaoqing Zhang, Ang Lv, Yuhan Liu, Flood Sung, Wei Liu, Jian Luan, Shuo Shang, Xiuying Chen, Rui Yan
    ACL 2025 CCF A类
  • Analysing The Impact of Sequence Composition on Language Model Pre-Training
    Yu Zhao, Yuanbin Qu, Konrad Staniszewski, Szymon Tworkowski, Wei Liu, Piotr Miłoś, Yuxiang Wu, Pasquale Minervini
    ACL 2024 CCF A类 Oral
  • A Comprehensive Evaluation of Quantization Strategies for Large Language Models
    Renren Jin, Jiangcun Du, Wuwei Huang, Wei Liu, Jian Luan, Bin Wang, Deyi Xiong
    ACL 2024 CCF A类

    系统性评估多种LLM量化策略（包括GPTQ、SpQR等），从效率、知识容量和一致性三个维度分析量化对模型性能的影响。

  • Stability and Generalization of Zeroth-Order Decentralized Stochastic Gradient Descent with Changing Topology
    Xiaolin Hu, Zixuan Gong, Gengze Xu, Wei Liu, Jian Luan, Bin Wang, Yong Liu
    AAAI 2025 CCF A类 Oral
大模型智能体（AI Agent）

强化学习、GUI Agent、工具调用、RAG、智能体推理与决策

  • Browsing Like Human: A Multimodal Web Agent with Experiential Fast-and-Slow Thinking
    Haohao Luo, Jiayi Kuang, Wei Liu, Ying Shen, Jian Luan, Yang Deng
    ACL 2025 CCF A类

    提出WebExperT框架，模拟人类"快慢思考"的规划过程，利用经验式学习从失败中反思，在MIND2WEB基准测试中表现卓越。

  • ReachAgent: Enhancing Mobile Agent via Page Reaching and Page Operation
    Qinzhuo Wu, Wei Liu, Jian Luan, Bin Wang
    NAACL 2025 CCF A类

    提出两阶段框架，将手机控制任务拆解为页面到达和页面操作子任务，任务完成率提升4.72%，在AutoUI数据集上超越SOTA。

  • MobileSteward: Integrating Multiple App-Oriented Agents with Self-Evolution to Automate Cross-App Instructions
    Yuxuan Liu, Hongda Sun, Wei Liu, Jian Luan, Bo Du, Rui Yan
    KDD 2025 CCF A类

    集成多个面向应用的Agent，通过自进化机制实现跨应用指令自动化，显著提升复杂任务执行效率。

  • Mobile-Bench: An Evaluation Benchmark for LLM-based Mobile Agents
    Shihan Deng, Weikai Xu, Hongda Sun, Wei Liu, Tao Tan, Jianfeng Liu, Ang Li, Jian Luan, Bin Wang, Rui Yan, Shuo Shang
    EMNLP 2025 CCF B类
  • MobileVLM: A Vision-Language Model for Better Intra- and Inter-UI Understanding
    Qinzhuo Wu, Weikai Xu, Wei Liu, Tao Tan, Jianfeng Liu, Ang Li, Jian Luan, Bin Wang, Shuo Shang
    EMNLP 2024 CCF B类

    构建300万UI页面的Mobile3M数据集，提出增强UI内和UI间理解的预训练任务，在骁龙888 CPU上达到行业领先推理速度。
